How it works

Built to be honest first: nothing the model sees could not have been known before kickoff, and the betting line is never an input.

01 · RATINGS

Opponent-adjusted efficiency

Every week, 21 offense and defense ratings are refit from play-by-play (EPA, success rate, explosive plays, pressure, red zone, special teams, pace…), adjusted for who each team played. Garbage time is ignored.

02 · PLAYERS

Quarterbacks & rosters

Each starting QB is rated by EPA per dropback and ESPN QBR. Every other player is rated by his contract's share of the cap, weighted by snaps and by the official injury report. ESPN fantasy projections add a second opinion.

03 · MODEL

Ridge + trees, retrained weekly

Several model recipes compete; the live one only changes when a challenger is clearly better on recent seasons. Totals add weather: wind matters most.

04 · TESTING

An honest backtest

Settings were chosen on early seasons only, then scored on later seasons the model never saw. The model is close to the closing line but slightly behind it, and no betting strategy has proven an edge.

05 · UPDATES

Runs itself

Full refresh every Tuesday. Injury reports, rosters and projections are pulled again Thursday, Saturday and Sunday morning, and games that haven't kicked off are re-predicted.
